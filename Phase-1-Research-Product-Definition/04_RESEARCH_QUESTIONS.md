# Research Questions

## RQ1
Can a coordinated multi-agent workflow produce a more comprehensive and actionable software-quality assessment than an uncoordinated single AI workflow?

## RQ2
How accurately can the system identify and prioritize software-quality issues when AI interpretation is grounded in deterministic analyzer evidence?

## RQ3
Can AI-assisted repair recommendations improve selected software-quality metrics without introducing regressions?

## RQ4
Can the unified workflow reduce human effort required to interpret and act on software-quality findings?

## Hypothesis Direction
The project should formulate null and alternative hypotheses after baseline tools, datasets, and metrics are finalized. Do not claim superiority before experimentation.
