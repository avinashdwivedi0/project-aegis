# Research Gap

## Working Gap
Existing software-quality workflows commonly combine specialized deterministic tools but leave developers to manually interpret fragmented findings. AI coding systems can explain or modify code but do not necessarily provide a reproducible, evidence-grounded, multi-dimensional quality workflow.

## Proposed Research Direction
Study whether a coordinated multi-agent layer can integrate objective analysis evidence, correlate and prioritize findings, provide explanations, and generate reviewable repair recommendations within one workflow.

## Important Limitation
This is a working gap, not a final literature claim. It must be validated and refined through the literature survey.

## Expected Contribution
A reproducible prototype and empirical evaluation of an evidence-grounded multi-agent software-quality workflow.
