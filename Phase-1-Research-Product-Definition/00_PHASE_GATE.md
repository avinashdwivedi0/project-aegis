# Phase 1 — Research & Product Definition

## Goal
Establish the research problem, product purpose, scope, requirements, and measurable evaluation direction before implementation.

## Deliverables
- Project Charter
- PRD
- TRD
- Research Questions
- Literature Review Plan
- Research Gap
- Problem Statement
- Objectives
- Scope
- Feasibility Study
- Requirements
- Evaluation Plan

## Exit Criteria
- Research problem is precise.
- Research gap is supported by literature.
- Scope is realistic for one student.
- Requirements are testable.
- Research questions have measurable evaluation methods.
- Version 1 scope is frozen.

## Gate
No production implementation begins until the Phase 1 baseline is reviewed and approved.
