# Research Paper Structure

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Background
4. Related Work
5. Research Gap
6. Research Questions
7. Proposed Framework
8. System Architecture
9. Methodology
10. Experimental Setup
11. Results
12. Discussion
13. Threats to Validity
14. Limitations
15. Conclusion
16. Future Work
17. References

Every result must be traceable to an experiment.
