# Metrics

## Detection
Precision, recall, F1 where ground truth exists.

## Repair
Patch acceptance, test pass rate, regression rate, semantic correctness where independently assessable.

## Efficiency
Analysis duration, human review time, token/cost usage.

## Quality
Before/after analyzer results and carefully defined quality scores.

## AI
Evidence grounding, factual correctness, schema compliance, usefulness.

No metric is valid merely because the platform reports it.
