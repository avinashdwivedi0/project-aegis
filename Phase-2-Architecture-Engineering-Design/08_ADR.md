# Architecture Decision Records

## ADR-001 Evidence-first AI
Decision: deterministic analyzers provide objective evidence; AI interprets it.

## ADR-002 Human approval
Decision: consequential code changes require explicit approval.

## ADR-003 Isolated execution
Decision: repositories execute in sandboxed workers.

## ADR-004 Provider abstraction
Decision: AI provider/model selection is abstracted from business logic.

## ADR-005 Versioned experiments
Decision: models, prompts, tools, scoring, and configurations are versioned for reproducibility.

Future decisions must record context, alternatives, decision, consequences, and status.
